RivoCaption — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. Publisher: RibhLab / Android APPS Creator.

RivoCaption is an Android video-caption editor. This policy explains how the app handles imported media, audio, subtitles, downloaded language models, optional online features, and advertising.

1. Media and captions

You choose which videos or media to import using Android's file picker. The app processes supported media and generates or edits captions on the device when you select local processing. Projects, imported copies, speech models, and caption files may be stored in the app's private device storage. Files you intentionally export or share are saved to the location or app you choose.

The publisher does not automatically receive copies of your videos, audio, or caption projects during normal on-device editing.

2. Internet access and speech models

RivoCaption can download speech-recognition and translation models when you ask for them. A connection to a model host or Google ML Kit services may disclose technical connection data such as your IP address to that provider. Downloading models can continue using Android's foreground data-sync service and its associated notifications where supported. Offline caption processing does not require uploading your video to a server.

3. Optional cloud processing

The app includes a user-consent design for optional cloud processing. If a cloud service is configured and you explicitly choose and approve it, the selected video may be sent over HTTPS to that service for processing. The app does not enable this upload silently. At the time of this policy, the production cloud processing endpoint is not configured, so this feature is not available for general use.

4. Advertising and consent

The Android app integrates Google Mobile Ads and Google's consent-management tools. When advertising is available, Google and its partners may process advertising identifiers, device or app information, usage signals, approximate location derived from IP address, and diagnostics in accordance with applicable consent choices and Google's privacy terms. You can use any privacy-options control offered in the app, where required, and your device's advertising settings. Learn about Google's practices at Google Privacy Policy.

5. Data retention and control

No RivoCaption account is required for basic local editing. On-device projects and downloaded models remain on your device until removed through supported app or system controls or the app is uninstalled. Exported files and files shared to other apps are outside the app's private storage; you must remove those separately. Data handled by advertising, model-hosting or other third-party services is subject to those providers' respective policies.

6. Permissions

Media access is used to open media you choose. Internet access supports optional model downloads and advertising. Notification and foreground service permissions may be used to inform you about user-initiated downloads and long-running tasks. The app does not need access to your contacts to generate captions.

7. Security and policy updates

We seek to limit data transmission and use Android platform security controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy when app features, service providers or legal requirements change, and will update the date above.
